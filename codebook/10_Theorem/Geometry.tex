\begin{itemize}
\item Pick's theorem

Simple polygon with lattice vertices: $A = I + \frac{B}{2} - 1$ ($I$: interior lattice points, $B$: boundary lattice points). $B = \sum_{\text{edges}} \gcd(|\Delta x|, |\Delta y|)$. Region with $h$ polygonal holes (disjoint from each other and from the outer boundary): $A = I + \frac{B}{2} + h - 1$.

\item Spherical cap (a portion of a sphere cut off by a plane)

\begin{itemize}
  \item $r$: sphere radius, $a$: radius of the base of the cap, $h$: height of the cap, $\cos\theta = (r-h)/r$.
  \item Volume $=\pi h^2(3r-h)/3=\pi h(3a^2+h^2)/6=\pi r^3(2+\cos\theta)(1-\cos\theta)^2/3$.
  \item Area $=2\pi rh=\pi(a^2+h^2)=2\pi r^2(1-\cos\theta)$.
\end{itemize}

\item Lagrange multiplier

To optimize $f(x_1, \ldots, x_n)$ subject to $g_i(x) = 0$ ($1 \le i \le k$): at an optimum (with linearly independent $\nabla g_i$), $\nabla f = \sum_i \lambda_i \nabla g_i$. Solve together with all $g_i = 0$; check the boundary separately.
